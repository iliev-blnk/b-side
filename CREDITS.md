# Credits

## Samples

The instrument samples in `public/samples` come from two libraries by Versilian Studios, both under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):

- the [Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL)
- the [Versilian Studios Chamber Orchestra 2, Community Edition (VSCO-2 CE)](https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE)

`scripts/samples.mjs` trimmed them, made them mono, peak-normalized them and converted them to mp3.
