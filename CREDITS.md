# Credits

## Samples

All instrument samples in `public/samples` come from the
[Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) by Versilian Studios,
released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They were trimmed, made
mono, peak-normalized and converted to mp3 by `scripts/samples.mjs`.

| Part | VCSL folder |
|---|---|
| piano | Chordophones/Zithers/Upright Piano, Knight |
| keys | Electrophones/TX81Z/FM Piano |
| sax | Aerophones/Reed Aerophones/Tenor Saxophone (non-vibrato) |
| vibes | Idiophones/Struck Idiophones/Vibraphone (soft mallets) |
| harmonica | Aerophones/Free Aerophones/Harmonica-Hohner-Special20-C |
| hat | Idiophones/Struck Idiophones/Hi-Hat Cymbal |
| snare | Membranophones/Struck Membranophones/Snare Drum, Modern 1 |
| shaker | Idiophones/Struck Idiophones/Shaker, Small |

Kick, bass and vinyl crackle are synthesized in the browser.
